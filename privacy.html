Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Solrise Weather ("Solrise", "we") is a weather app for iPhone made by CU Adventures LLC. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app uses and why. The short version: we use your location only to show you weather, it stays on your iPhone, and we never sell it. There are no ads, no tracking, and no analytics in the app.

What we collect

Location. With your permission, Solrise reads your current location to show weather where you are. You can also add cities by searching instead of sharing your location. Your saved cities, including their coordinates, are stored only on your iPhone.

Preferences. Settings like units, card order, and which advice lens you prefer are stored only on your iPhone.

Purchases. Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your purchase is active.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or any account information. The app has no sign-in.

Who the app talks to

To get weather for a place, Solrise sends that place's coordinates (not your name or any identifier) to the weather services below. Like any website or server, these services may briefly log technical request information such as your IP address.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Apple WeatherKitCoordinatesForecast, current conditions, alerts
Solrise backend (hosted on Vercel), which relays to ForecaCoordinates, map tile positionRadar, air quality, thunder chance, snow depth, sunshine
National Weather Service (NOAA)Coordinates (US places)Alert details, snow level
NOAA Aviation Weather CenterAn area around the placeNearest airport reports (METAR, TAF)
NOAA Tides & CurrentsThe nearest station IDTides and coastal conditions
NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center and Storm Prediction CenterNothing about you (public data feeds)Aurora, solar activity, storm outlooks
Apple MapsThe map area you viewMap display and city search

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't build a profile of you.

Your choices

You can turn location access off any time in iPhone Settings, then Solrise, then Location, and add cities by search instead. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Children

Solrise is a general-audience weather app and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
