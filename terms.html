Free and Premium

Solrise is free to download and use. The free version includes the forecast, current conditions, every core reading with its explanation, your location plus 2 saved cities, and the small Home Screen widget.

Solrise Premium unlocks extra features, including live radar and map layers, unlimited saved cities, the medium, large, and Lock Screen widgets, sky conditions for pilots and drone operators, and the full guide in each reading (the next 24 hours, why it moves, and advice for everyday, outdoors, and flying). Every way of buying Premium unlocks exactly the same features.

Ways to buy Premium

  • Monthly: $2.49 per month, renews automatically every month.
  • Yearly: $14.99 per year, renews automatically every year. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial.
  • Lifetime: $34.99, paid once. It never renews and there is nothing to cancel.

Prices are in US dollars. Prices in other countries are set by the App Store in local currency and may include taxes. The price shown in the app at the moment you buy is the price you pay.

How subscriptions work

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase.
  • A subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your Apple ID is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period, at the same price unless we tell you about a change in advance as the App Store requires.
  • With the yearly free trial, you won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the 7 days end. If you buy another plan during the trial, any unused part of the trial ends.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription any time in iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Solrise Weather. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Premium stays on until the end of the period you already paid for.

Lifetime purchase

Lifetime is a one-time purchase that unlocks Premium for as long as Solrise Weather is offered on the App Store. It is tied to your Apple ID, so it carries over to a new iPhone: tap Restore on the Upgrade screen if it doesn't come back on its own.

Weather information

Solrise shows forecasts and readings from Apple Weather, Foreca, and NOAA. Weather is uncertain: forecasts can be wrong, data can be delayed, and a source can be unavailable. Please use Solrise as a helpful guide, not as your only source for decisions where safety is at stake.

  • In dangerous weather, follow official warnings and instructions from your national weather service and local authorities.
  • Sky conditions, flight categories, and the decoded METAR and TAF reports are for information and learning. They are not an official preflight weather briefing and don't replace one. Pilots and drone operators remain responsible for checking official sources before flying.
  • Explanations and advice in the app are general information, not professional, medical, or safety advice.
