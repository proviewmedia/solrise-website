Solrise Weather. Weather that explains itself.

Solrise Weather is a weather app for iPhone that explains every reading. Alongside live rain radar worldwide and clean 24-hour and 7-day forecasts, every number, from UV index and air quality to wind, aurora, tides, and aviation METAR and TAF, opens a plain-language guide: what it means, where today sits on the real published scale, what it feels like, and what it means for everyday life, outdoor activities, or flying. There are no ads and no tracking.

Features

  • Live rain radar worldwide, with real radar across North America, Europe, East Asia, Australia, Brazil, and more, and a forecast model elsewhere
  • Hourly and 7-day forecast charts, with each night's moon phase
  • Minute-by-minute next-hour rain
  • Official severe weather alerts with full text, always free
  • US EPA air quality index and WHO UV index, with what each level means for your health
  • A guide for every reading: the published scale, what it feels like, a sourced fact, and advice for everyday, outdoors, and flying
  • Aurora forecast from the NOAA Kp index, tides from NOAA, and nearest-airport METAR and TAF decoded
  • Cloud, temperature, wind, and pressure maps, plus radar forecast playback (Premium)
  • A customizable Today screen, with every city shown on its own local clock
  • No ads, no tracking, no sign-in
